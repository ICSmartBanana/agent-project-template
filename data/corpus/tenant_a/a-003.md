# IT service desk guide

## Opening hours

The IT service desk is staffed from 07:00 to 19:00 on working days. Outside those hours, log a ticket and it is picked up the next morning.

## Password resets

Reset your own password through the NW-Reset self-service portal. The service desk does not reset passwords by phone.
