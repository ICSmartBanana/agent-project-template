# Supplier payment terms

## Standard terms

Northwind Logistics pays supplier invoices 45 days after the invoice date, unless a contract says otherwise.

## Disputes

A supplier invoice can be disputed within 10 business days of receipt. Raise the dispute with accounts payable and attach the purchase order.
