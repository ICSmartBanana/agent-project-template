# Expense claims policy

## Deadline

Northwind Logistics staff submit expense claims within 30 days of the expense date. Claims submitted later are paid only with finance director approval.

## Approval

Claims above 250 EUR need line-manager approval before finance pays them. Claims are submitted through the Expensify Hub portal with a receipt for every item.
